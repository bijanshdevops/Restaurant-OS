## Capability Overview

Restaurants OS delivers capabilities across 11 domains:

1. **Menu Management** — Design, version, and publish menus per brand and branch
2. **Order Management** — Full order lifecycle from creation to fulfilment
3. **Kitchen Operations** — Production tracking, routing, and offline resilience
4. **Table & Reservation Management** — Floor plan, reservations, and waitlist
5. **Financial Management** — Double-entry ledger, multi-currency, tax, royalties
6. **Inventory & Supply Chain** — Ingredients, recipes, procurement, suppliers
7. **Staff Management** — Scheduling, roles, attendance, payroll integration
8. **Guest Experience** — Profiles, loyalty, reservations, feedback
9. **Reporting & Business Intelligence** — Operational, financial, and franchise reporting
10. **Platform Services** — Identity, plugins, configuration, storage, notifications
11. **Franchise Management** — Onboarding, brand compliance, royalty calculation

See [Business Capability Map](06-business-capability-map.md) for full capability hierarchy with ownership.
